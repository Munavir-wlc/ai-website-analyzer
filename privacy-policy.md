# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the AI Website Analyzer platform collects, processes, and protects information when you submit URLs for scanning and security audits.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Scanned URLs and Target Domains
When you request a scan, we crawl and audit the target URL. We store:
- Crawled page HTML markup.
- Metadata, redirect paths, SSL certificates, DNS configurations, and open port mappings.
- Cookies, HTTP response headers, and exposed administrative files.

### B. User Account Data
If you register an account, we collect:
- Full Name.
- Business Email Address.
- Cryptographically hashed passwords.
- Billing logs (if you subscribe to premium tiers via Stripe).

## 2. How Data is Processed and Shared

### A. Scanning Infrastructure
We perform security mapping, performance measurements, and accessibility audits. This information is saved to our secure database to generate reports.

### B. AI Engine Processing
We utilize third-party Large Language Model APIs (e.g. OpenAI GPT-4) to compile explanations, summaries, and answering target questions. We sanitize target page contents before sending requests. We **never** share your credentials, cookies, authorization tokens, or user account metadata with AI providers.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
- Scanned reports are retained for your comparison history.
- You can delete any scan report from your dashboard at any time, which purges the document from our databases.
- You may close your account and request complete data purging by contacting support.
